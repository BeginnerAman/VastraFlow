Engineered for Apparel Retail

Built Specifically for Clothing Showrooms

Every feature in VastraFlow addresses a real-world problem faced on the shop floor: sizes, colors, barcode tags, customer exchanges, and udhar bahi-khata.

Lightning-Fast Counter POS

Scan barcodes or search products instantly. Designed for rush hours with keyboard hotkeys, cash drawer change calculation, and split payment modes (Cash, UPI, Card, Udhar).

  • Barcode Scanning
  • Split Payments
  • Item & Bill Discounts

Size and Color Variant Matrix

One product, multiple stockable units. Track exact stock per size (38, 40, 42, XL, XXL) and color (Blue, Olive, Maroon) with individual reorder thresholds.

  • SKU Auto-Generation
  • Low Stock Alerts
  • Dead Stock Analysis

Garment Barcode Tag Printing

Generate and print crisp Code-128 barcode stickers ready for standard label printers. Print single tags or batch print entire lots with your shop name, size, and MRP.

  • 50x25mm Label Ready
  • Custom Shop Header
  • SVG Vector Barcodes

Thermal POS & A4 PDF Invoices

Support for high-speed 80mm thermal receipts with auto-cut, plus publication-quality A4 tax invoices with custom shop headers, GSTIN, and return policies.

  • 80mm / 58mm Thermal
  • ReportLab A4 PDF
  • Direct Windows Print

Returns and Size Exchanges

Process customer returns and size exchanges in a single transaction. Automatic restock or damage classification with zero-drift proportional discount reconciliation.

  • Restock vs Damaged
  • Single-Bill Exchange
  • Refund / Credit Notes

Staff Roles & Price Privacy

Separate Admin and Cashier accounts. Counter staff can bill and check stock, but wholesale purchase costs and supplier debts remain strictly hidden.

  • Owner vs Staff Role
  • Cost Price Masking
  • Security Audit Logs

Plug and Play

Hardware You Can Use Today

No special proprietary hardware required. VastraFlow works seamlessly with your existing desktop, laptop, thermal printers, and barcode scanners.

Barcode Scanners

Any standard USB or Wireless 1D/2D handheld barcode scanner. Just plug in and scan.

Thermal POS Printers

Standard 80mm (3-inch) or 58mm (2-inch) thermal receipt printers (TVS, Epson, NGX, POSIFLEX).

Laser and Inkjet Printers

Any regular A4 or A5 desktop printer (HP, Canon, Brother, Epson) for crisp full-page tax invoices.

Windows Computers

Runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). Works smoothly on budget laptops and POS terminals.
