Core Capabilities

Designed for Apparel Showroom Operations

Every tool in VastraFlow addresses a practical operational challenge: garment sizes, color variations, barcode tag printing, size exchanges, and supplier dues.

Fast Counter POS Billing

Scan barcodes or search products instantly. Designed for busy checkout lines with keyboard hotkeys, cash drawer change calculation, and split payment options across Cash, UPI, Card, and Store Credit.

  • Barcode Scanning
  • Split Payments
  • Item & Bill Discounts

Size and Color Variant Hierarchy

One catalog entry, multiple stock units. Track exact stock levels for sizes (38, 40, 42, Free Size) and colors (Blue, Olive, Black) with individual reorder points and automated low-stock warnings.

  • Automated SKU Format
  • Reorder Thresholds
  • Dead Stock Tracking

Garment Barcode Tag Printing

Create and print crisp Code-128 barcode stickers ready for standard roll label printers. Print single replacement tags or batch print entire new inward lots with shop name, size, and MRP.

  • 50x25mm Label Format
  • Custom Shop Header
  • Vector Barcodes

Receipts & Publication-Quality Invoices

Integrated support for fast 80mm and 58mm thermal counter receipts, alongside formal A4 tax invoices with store branding, GSTIN, customer details, and store return policies.

  • 80mm POS Thermal
  • ReportLab A4 PDF
  • Direct Windows Spooling

Returns and Size Exchanges

Process customer returns and size replacements in a single transaction. Items are classified as restocked or damaged, with exact proportional discount math and automated debt adjustment.

  • Restock vs Damaged Goods
  • Single-Bill Exchange
  • Credit Settlement

Staff Roles & Cost Price Privacy

Independent Administrator and Cashier accounts. Staff can complete sales and check available stock, while confidential wholesale purchase costs and supplier dues remain completely hidden.

  • Owner vs Cashier Access
  • Wholesale Cost Masking
  • Security Audit Records

Hardware Support

Hardware You Already Own

No specialized or proprietary hardware required. VastraFlow works directly with standard PC peripherals, barcode scanners, and thermal receipt printers.

Barcode Scanners

Standard USB or wireless handheld and omnidirectional 1D/2D barcode scanners. Ready to scan upon connection.

Thermal POS Printers

Standard 80mm (3-inch) or 58mm (2-inch) thermal receipt printers from TVS, Epson, NGX, POSIFLEX, and others.

Laser and Inkjet Printers

Standard desktop A4 or A5 printers from HP, Canon, Brother, and Epson for formal tax invoices and stock sheets.

Windows Computers

Compatible with Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). Operates smoothly on desktop workstations and budget laptops.
