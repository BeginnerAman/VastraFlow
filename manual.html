Section 01

VastraFlow Kya Hai & Kaise Chalta Hai?

VastraFlow ek complete modern desktop management software hai jo khas taur par kapdon (Garments, Apparel, Fashion Retail & Family Stores) ke hisab-kitab aur fast counter billing ke liye banaya gaya hai.

1
Desktop Window App (Offline First)
Ye software bina internet ke bhi 100% offline kaam karta hai. Data aapke computer mein hi surakshit rehta hai, kisi cloud ya external server par depend nahi karta.
2
Two User Roles: Admin vs Staff (Cashier)
Admin (Shop Owner): Sabhi reports, munafa (Profit/Loss), kharid bhav, manual stock change, aur dukan settings dekh sakta hai.
Staff (Cashier/Counter Boy): Sirf billing counter, sales history, customer return, aur stock count dekh sakta hai. Dukaan ka secret kharid bhav Staff ko nahi dikhta.
3
Smart Trial & Permanent Activation
Software shuruat mein 15-day smart trial mode mein chalta hai. Permanent activation ke liye Admin ➔ License & Activation menu se Hardware ID (HWID) copy karke official activation key paste kar sakte hain.
Pro-Tip for Staff: Roz subah dukan kholte hi application icon par double-click karein. Screen 2 second mein open ho jayegi aur directly billing counter par kaam shuru kiya ja sakta hai.

1

1. 7 dino ke andar bill ke saath exchange sambhav hai.
2. Bik chuka maal wapas nahi hoga, sirf badla jayega.
3. Dhule ya altered kapde exchange nahi honge.
Impact: Yeh settings ek baar save karne ke baad har thermal receipt slip aur A4 invoice par automatic header aur footer ban kar chhapne lagengi.

Section 04

Product & Variants Entry (Size, Color, Pricing)

Kapdon ki dukaan mein ek hi shirt ke alag-alag Size (38, 40, 42) aur Color (Blue, Black, White) hote hain. VastraFlow isme Parent Product aur Child Variants structure use karta hai.

1
Step 1: Main Product Banayein
Products ➔ All Products ➔ + Add Product par jayein.
• Product Name: Cotton Casual Shirt
• Category: Men Shirts | Brand: Allen Solly
• HSN Code (Optional): Jaise 6205. Product image upload kar sakte hain. Save karein.
2
Step 2: Variants (Size & Color) Jodein
Product open karke + Add Variant dabayein:
• Size: M, L, XL, 32, 34, Free Size
• Colour: Sky Blue, Jet Black, Navy
• SKU / Barcode: Ek unique code (jaise: AS-SHT-BLU-M) ya kapde par laga barcode number scan karein.
• Reorder Point: Minimum stock limit (default: 5 piece) taaki khatam hone par alert mile.
3
Step 3: Three-Tier Pricing (Teen Rate System)
1. Purchase Cost (₹): Dukaan ko wholesale mein kitne ka pada (jaise ₹650).
2. MRP (₹): Tag par kitna chhapna hai (jaise ₹1,499).
3. Selling Price (₹): Counter par customer ko kitne mein bechna hai (jaise ₹1,199).
Bulk Excel Upload: Agar aapke paas 500 ya 1000 kapdon ki list hai, toh Products ➔ Download Template se Excel file download karein, usme data bharein aur Import Excel button se 10 second mein poora stock upload kar lein!

Wholesale Purchases (Dukaan mein Maal Aana / Inward)

Surat, Delhi, ya local dealer se jab kapdon ka naya parcel ya wholesale bill aata hai, toh uski entry yahan hoti hai taaki stock automatically badh jaye aur dealer ka hisab ban jaye.

1
Purchases ➔ New Purchase Order par jayein
• Supplier: Dealer chunein (jaise: Surat Textile Syndicate). Naya dealer ho toh wahin se bana sakte hain.
• Invoice Ref: Dealer ke bill ka number dalein (jaise: ST-8942).
• Purchase Date: Bill ki tareekh.
2
Kapdon ke Items aur Quantity Dalein
Jo-jo items parcel mein aaye hain unhe list mein chunein, kitne piece aaye (Quantity) aur per piece kya rate pada (Cost Price) dalein. Total amount system jod lega.
3
Payment & Due (Udhar) Status
• Amount Paid: Agar aapne abhi dealer ko kuch paisa de diya hai (e.g. ₹10,000) toh wo dalein.
• Baki Amount: Jo paisa baki reh jayega, wo automatically us Supplier ke Khate (Balance Due) mein jud jayega.
Supplier Return (Debit Note): Agar parcel mein koi kapda kata-phata nikla ya rang utra hua aaya, toh Purchases ➔ Purchase Returns se dealer ko wapas bhej sakte hain. Utna stock minus ho jayega aur dealer ke khate se paisa kam ho jayega.

Section 09

Customer Returns & Size Exchanges

Kapdon ki dukaan mein customer size chota ya bada hone par badalwane zaroor aata hai. VastraFlow mein return aur exchange dono 1-minute mein ho jate hain.

1
Bill Number Lookup Karein (Sales ➔ Customer Returns)
Customer ke bill par chhapa invoice number dalein (jaise: INV-202610-0012) aur Find Invoice dabayein.
2
Case A: Item Wapas Lena (Return)
Jo kapda wapas aaya hai uski quantity chunein (e.g. 1 piece). System turant utna stock wapas inventory mein jod lega (`RETURN_IN`) aur customer ko diye jane wale refund ka hisab bana dega.
3
Case B: Size Badalna (Exchange)
Customer Size 'M' dekar Size 'L' le raha hai:
• Purane item ko Return mein dalein.
• Naye item ko Replacement mein dalein.
• Math Difference: Agar dono ka rate same hai toh settlement ₹0 hoga. Agar naya item mehnga hai toh customer se baki paisa collect hoga; agar sasta hai toh customer ko refund diya jayega.
Section 10

Inventory Control, Stock Adjustment & Dead Stock

Dukaan mein kitna maal bacha hai, kaunsa khatam hone wala hai aur kaunsa kapda salon se nahi bika, ye sab yahan dekha jata hai.

Feature Screen Kya Kaam Aata Hai? Kisko Use Karna Chahiye?
Stock Overview (`/inventory`) Live stock count, size-wise stock dekhna, out-of-stock items ki list. Admin aur Staff dono dekh sakte hain.
Reorder Alerts (`/inventory/reorder`) Jo kapde minimum level se kam bache hain, unki auto-list taaki naya order diya ja sake. Shop Owner (Admin) purchase order banane ke liye.
Manual Stock Adjustment (`/inventory/adjust`) Agar physically ginne par 1 piece kam ya jyada nikle (chori, damage, sample, counting diff). Mandatory reason ke saath stock modify kiya jata hai. Sirf Admin kar sakta hai (Security Audit Lock).
Dead Stock Report (`/inventory/dead-stock`) Jo kapde 30, 60, ya 90 dino se bilkul nahi biken. Unpar discount sale lagane ke liye. Shop Owner (Admin).
Valuation Report (`/inventory/valuation`) Dukaan mein rakhe poore stock ki aaj ki total kharid kimat (WAC / FIFO / Latest Cost). Shop Owner (Admin) for Net Worth & Balance Sheet.

Customer & Supplier Khata (Udhar & Payment Ledger)

Kisi customer par kitna udhar baki hai aur kis wholesale dealer ko kitna payment dena hai, iska poora digital khata VastraFlow maintain karta hai.

1
Customer Khata (Grahak ka Udhar)
• Customers menu mein jayein. Jis customer par baki hai unke naam ke samne Due: ₹X,XXX red color mein dikhega.
• Jab customer aakar paisa jama kare, toh unke profile par Record Payment dabayein.
• Amount dalein (e.g. ₹2,000) aur payment method (Cash ya UPI) select karein. System unka balance kam kar dega aur payment receipt generate kar dega.
2
Supplier Khata (Dealer ka Payment)
• Suppliers menu mein dealer ka profile kholein.
• Unke sabhi purane purchase bills aur unpaid balance ki list dikhegi.
• Jab aap dealer ko RTGS/NEFT/Cheque ya Cash se payment karein, toh Record Supplier Payment dabakar entry karein taaki unka khata clear ho sake.

Section 16

Frequently Asked Questions (Aam Sawal-Jawab & Solutions)

Q1: Agar barcode scanner se scan na ho toh kya karein?
Bheed ke samay ghabrayein nahi! Keyboard par F4 ya / dabayein aur kapde ka naam ya size type karein (e.g. White Shirt 40). Dropdown se select karke Enter dabayein, item turant add ho jayega. Barcode scanner ki USB wire bhi check kar lein.
Q2: Kya cashier shop ke kharid bhav (Cost Price) ya munafa dekh sakta hai?
Nahi! Agar cashier ka account 'Staff' role par bana hai, toh use kharid bhav, profit margins, aur P&L reports bilkul nahi dikhte. Staff ko sirf MRP, Selling Price, aur Billing access hota hai.
Q3: Galat bill ban gaya ya customer ne lene se mana kar diya, toh kya karein?
Sales ➔ Customer Returns mein jayein, us bill ka invoice number dalein aur item ko Return select karein. Utna stock turant dukaan ke stock mein wapas jud jayega aur cash balance adjust ho jayega.
Q4: 15-day trial khatam hone par kya mera data delete ho jayega?
Nahi, bilkul nahi! Trial khatam hone par software Read-Only mode mein switch ho jata hai taaki aapka ek bhi bill ya stock data loose na ho. Admin ➔ License & Activation screen par jakar official license key enter karte hi software wapas full functionality mein activate ho jata hai.
Q5: Naye computer par shift karna ho toh kaise karein?
Purane computer se Admin ➔ Database Backups ➔ Create Backup karein aur banayi gayi .db file ko Pen Drive mein le lein. Naye computer mein VastraFlow install karke us backup file ko restore kar lein. Aapka 100% data naye PC par chalu ho jayega.
