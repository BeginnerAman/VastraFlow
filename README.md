## Key Highlights

* 100% Offline Architecture: Runs locally on your Windows PC. No active internet connection required for billing, stock queries, or daily closing.
* Apparel Variant Matrix: Native handling of size (38, 40, 42, Free Size) and color combinations under single parent products.
* Garment Barcode Tag Printing: Print customized adhesive barcode tag stickers with shop branding, size, and MRP ready for clothing items.
* Rapid Counter POS: Keyboard-first counter billing with barcode scanning, split payments across Cash, UPI, Card, and Store Credit, plus instant change calculation.
* Dual Invoicing Formats: High-speed 80mm thermal receipt format with barcode, plus publication-quality A4 tax invoice PDF generation.
* Customer Store Credit Ledger: Real-time customer credit tracking with chronological payment histories and zero double-counting.
* Customer Returns and Size Exchanges: Restock vs damage classification and single-bill size exchanges with exact proportional discount settling.
* Wholesale Price Privacy: Strict role-based permissions (Shop Owner vs Cashier Staff). Wholesale costs and supplier dues are hidden from counter staff.
* Accrual Profit and Loss Engine: Mathematical separation between Sales Revenue, Cash Collections, and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).
* 15-Day Free Trial and Safe Soft-Lock: Includes an unrestricted 15-day trial. If the trial expires, past bills, reports, and stock data are never locked.

## Quick Start Guide

1. Download the latest installer: [VastraFlow_Setup_v3.0.exe](https://github.com/BeginnerAman/VastraFlow/releases/download/v3.0.0/VastraFlow_Setup_v3.0.exe).
2. Double-click to launch the setup wizard.
3. Follow the simple on-screen instructions (no administrative UAC elevation prompt required).
4. Launch VastraFlow from the desktop shortcut.
5. Create your initial Shop Owner account and begin your 15-day free trial.

## Fast Counter Keyboard Shortcuts

* F2 : New Sale Counter Billing
* F4 : Add Product Line Row
* F6 : Focus Customer Selector
* F8 : Settle Full Cash
* F9 : Settle Full UPI
* Ctrl + P : Print Invoice / Thermal Receipt
* Ctrl + F : Quick Variant Search
* Esc : Close / Cancel Active Dialog
